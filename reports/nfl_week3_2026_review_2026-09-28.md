## Main Findings

1. Rushing selection was the largest loss source. Overs went 3-13, unders 13-19. The mean selected probability was 55.3%, but only 33.3% won. Slightly better yardage MAE did not translate to reliable line probabilities or selection.
2. Passing-yard unders went 6-13; overs went 3-0. Passing forecasts underpredicted by 36.1 yards on average. Large misses included Sam Darnold 181.3 projected versus 379 actual and Matthew Stafford 269.8 versus 390. Three overs are too few to establish an over strategy.
3. Receiving was modestly positive in the broad card cohort, with slightly better MAE and Brier than its baselines, but the fixed research selections failed. Improvements across all offers have not established improvements in the highest-priority selections.
4. Receiving tail misses remain: Drake London 42.7 projected versus 194 actual, Jordan Addison 14.6 versus 90, Brock Bowers 44.5 versus 116. These need workload/efficiency attribution, not automatic upward adjustments after one game.
5. Game picks used market-baseline fallback projections. All 15 spread forecasts had 50% side probability and matched their baseline margin. Their record is not evidence of an independently learned spread edge.
6. Recent exact closes were available for 159 of these 162 settled priced props (98.1%). Good capture is useful, but does not repair losing probabilities. The remaining three closes are unknown, not flat CLV.

No production models, selection rules, original locks, or cash approvals were changed for this review. A single week cannot establish profitability or justify a new model by itself.
