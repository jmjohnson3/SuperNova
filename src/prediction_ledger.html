What is counted?

    Repeated decisions keep the first saved pick within a strategy, version, sportsbook, player-game and market. Stat-only forecasts keep the last saved pregame revision. Later forecasts cannot improve an earlier locked pick's record. A model direction derived from a saved forecast is labeled as a diagnostic, not as a delivered recommendation. “Actual” for spreads and winner forecasts is the home team's winning margin.
